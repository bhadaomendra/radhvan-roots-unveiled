# Radhvan Origins: Cordyceps Unveiled

Design a completely original, premium, educational website for Radhvan Origins focused on Cordyceps. The website should combine the elegance of Apple, the storytelling of National Geographic, and the credibility of a modern biotechnology institute. It must not resemble any existing Cordyceps or herbal website. Use the brand colors (#F67304, #763C03, #2E3B11, #F9F8F3) and the provided Radhvan Origins logo as inspiration. Create a clean, responsive, fast-loading experience with generous white space, smooth micro-animations, custom AI-generated botanical and scientific illustrations, premium typography, and interactive educational elements. Keep the content concise, highly informative, and engaging—each section should be readable in under one minute while encouraging exploration. Include a cinematic hero section, What is Cordyceps, Why It Is Unique, Key Bioactive Compounds, Evidence-Based Benefits (with appropriate scientific disclaimers), Processing Overview, Research Highlights, About Radhvan Origins, Future Product Range, FAQ, and Newsletter. Avoid copied text, stock photography, or generic layouts. All illustrations, icons, and graphics must be original and tailored to the Radhvan Origins brand. The website should establish trust, educate visitors, and be future-ready for eCommerce while maintaining a handcrafted, premium design aesthetic.
